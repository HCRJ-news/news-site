Tell someone what happened, without giving your name

If you experienced sexual abuse, assault or harassment connected to a company, school, religious organization or government agency, you can report it here. Your report goes confidentially to the Hollywood Council for Restorative Justice.

If you are in danger right now

Call 911. This form is not monitored around the clock and does not contact police.

How your privacy is protected

  • We never ask for your name. Contact details are optional.
  • Reports are not saved on this website and are not published here.
  • Your report is sent over an encrypted connection and delivered only to the Hollywood Council for Restorative Justice. This site doesn't record your IP address.
  • To help us understand how people find this site, the report includes the name of the website that sent you here (for example, a search engine) and the first page you opened on this site. It never includes your search terms or browsing history.
  • A Cloudflare security check on this page blocks automated spam. It doesn't ask for any personal information.
  • To stay anonymous, leave out details that would identify you, such as your name, job title or the exact date.

What this is and isn't

Reporting here does not file a police report or a lawsuit, and it doesn't create an attorney-client relationship. If you want to report a crime, contact your local police. Time limits for lawsuits vary by state, so talk to a lawyer if you're considering legal action.

For free, confidential support 24/7, call the RAINN National Sexual Assault Hotline at 800-656-4673.
